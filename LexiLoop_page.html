LexiLoop

A supportive learning space for adults with dyslexia and learning differences. Through tutors, videos, community events, and personalized resources, the app helps users build confidence, learn at their own pace, and feel understood every step of the way.

App Design Project

This project was completed with Jordan Mak and Rita McDonald during the Fall 2025 term in the Interaction Design program at Capilano University for our Visual Design course. Designed for adults with dyslexia and learning differences, the app focuses on accessible, personalized support. Users can customize their experience through editable resource widgets, explore local support and events through an integrated map, and connect with others in a community forum for shared questions and learning.
