WordPress

“Out of Office” was a WordPress project I completed at the beginning of 2026, focused on creating a functional and stylish website for a fictional restaurant using the Divi page builder. With an emphasis on SEO, thoughtful content planning, and building fluency in WordPress, I dedicated significant time and effort to developing both my technical skills and overall design approach.

Out of Office

When designing “Out of Office,” I set out to create a chic, current wine bar that I could genuinely see myself unwinding in after a long day at the office. Guided by a post-work mindset, I envisioned it as a welcoming third place for friends, coworkers, and even clients to gather, relax, and “clock out and pour up.” The final site includes four fully developed pages: Home, About, Menu, and Reservations, each designed to feel cohesive, inviting, and easy to navigate.
